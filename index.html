Overview

Pendulum period

Does a pendulum's period change as its swing gets smaller? This method times a pendulum at a large and at a small swing, compares the two periods, and revises the starting hypothesis.

Method

  • Measurement

Performers

assistant
kind: human; role: laboratory assistant. Stages: Prepare, Observe
researcher
kind: human; role: scientist. Stages: Compare

Hypotheses

shorter-swing
A smaller swing has a shorter period.

Assumptions

hand-timing
Hand timing is comparable across trials.

Conclusions

regular-period
The period stayed nearly constant as the swing got smaller.

Requirements

  • Stages run in this order: Prepare → Observe → Compare → Revise.
